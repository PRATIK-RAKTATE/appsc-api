1Project Overview

APPSC Prep will be a student preparation and study-tracking platform for APPSC examination preparation. It will provide a responsive web application and an Android application for students and administrators.

The platform will combine bilingual e-book reading, online tests, performance analysis, mentor communication, courses, study materials, current affairs, and an AI assistant that can answer using approved learning content and enabled web sources.

Student Learning Workflow

StudyRead English and Telugu content
PracticeTake tests and quizzes
AnalyzeReview time and topic performance
ImproveUse mentors and AI guidance
ProgressTrack courses and weak topics
Core access rule: Students will use one active session at a time. Access to purchased courses and materials will be available on both web and Android according to the applicable validity period.








2What the System Will Include

Student Application

  • Email-based student login
  • Home dashboard and learning progress
  • Books, courses, materials, tests, current affairs, and AI access
  • Course enrolment after Razorpay payment
  • Validity-based access control
  • One active session per student

Admin Application

  • Admin login with role-based permissions
  • Manage students, mentors, books, materials, courses, tests, and current affairs
  • Approve and manage mentor accounts
  • Upload, update, approve, and remove content
  • View audit logs and manage platform settings
  • Backup and restore support

Bilingual E-Book Reading

Content and Conversion

  • English books to be provided with confirmed legal rights for digitization and display.
  • Books may be supplied as scanned content or other source material.
  • The technical digitization and content-processing workflow will be handled by the development team.
  • Telugu content will be generated using an automatic translation workflow and can be reviewed or updated by the admin.
  • English and Telugu will be displayed side-by-side for the same page or reading position.

Reader Features

  • Search within books and content
  • Bookmarks and reading progress
  • Highlights and personal notes
  • Font and reading-theme controls
  • Text-to-speech where technically supported
  • Download/offline reading only if separately approved for the selected content and security model

Online Tests and Performance Analysis

Area Included Scope
Test types Mock tests, sectional tests, previous-year papers, daily quizzes, and scheduled tests. The final combination can be configured as required.
Question management Admin-controlled question bank with subjects, topics, answers, explanations, schedules, and configurable test rules.
Configurable rules Negative marking, randomization, pause/resume, time limits, ranks, and leaderboards can be configured as per the approved requirement.
Analytics Score, time per question, accuracy, attempted/unattempted questions, topic-wise weakness, and recommendations.
Content responsibility: The question bank, explanations, subjects, topics, answers, test schedules, course content, and current-affairs content will be provided and managed by administrator users for ongoing content entry and updates.











3Mentoring, AI, Courses, and Current Affairs

Mentor Chat

  • One-to-one student-to-mentor chat
  • Topic-specific conversations
  • Text, file, image, voice-message, and link sharing
  • Chat history, moderation, and reporting
  • Mentor approval and assignment controlled by Admin

Smart AI Assistant

  • Answers based on approved books and study materials using RAG.
  • May use enabled web search and model capabilities for current or external information.
  • Source citations and book/page references in answers where available.
  • Admin-controlled content indexing and update workflow.
  • AI behavior and enabled sources remain subject to the selected model and service configuration.

Current Affairs and News

  • Admin-controlled current-affairs upload page.
  • Admin will upload news, papers, and related content through the admin panel.
  • RAG processing will make approved content available to the AI assistant.
  • Enabled web search may be used for relevant current information.
  • The system can identify and show relationships between new content and older content, subject to the approved matching behavior.

Courses and Materials

  • Course and material catalogue available on web and Android.
  • One-time purchase through Razorpay.
  • Validity-based access expiry.
  • Invoices, payment history, refunds, coupons, and access controls.
  • Video courses are included; live streaming classes are excluded.
  • Admin controls course and material publishing.

Content Uploads

Admin content tools will support PDF, scanned images, video, audio, and document uploads, subject to file-size, storage, security, and third-party service limits.

4Security, Roles, and Responsibilities

User Roles

Role Primary Responsibility
Student Read content, enrol in courses, take tests, view analytics, chat with approved mentors, and use the AI assistant.
Admin Manage students, mentors, content, books, tests, courses, payments, current affairs, permissions, moderation, audit logs, and platform settings.
Mentor Mentor access is subject to Admin approval. Mentor permissions and account workflow will be finalized in the detailed requirement.

A user cannot hold multiple roles. Role-based access will restrict each account to its permitted functions.

Application Security

  • Email login and secure authentication
  • One active session at a time
  • Role-based permissions
  • Admin audit logs
  • Backup and restore support
  • Protection against unauthorized access and content actions

Content Protection

  • Android screenshot and screen-recording restrictions where supported by the operating system.
  • Web security controls and best-effort browser restrictions.
  • Video and book access controlled by authentication and entitlement.

Responsibilities

Development Scope

  • Design and develop the web and Android applications
  • Build student, admin, mentor, content, test, payment, AI, and analytics features
  • Implement security, role permissions, audit logging, and one-session control
  • Configure the technical RAG and content-processing workflows
  • Test and provide production-ready application builds
  • Provide basic handover documentation

Content & Operational Scope

  • Provide English books and confirm digitization rights
  • Provide tests, courses, materials, current affairs, and other learning content
  • Perform content entry, proofreading, approval, and ongoing updates through Admin
  • Provide timely feedback, approvals, and test users/data
  • Provide required third-party accounts and service decisions

Acceptance Criteria

  1. Web and Android applications support approved student and admin workflows.
  2. Students can log in with email and only one active session is allowed.
  3. Books can be viewed with English and Telugu side-by-side according to approved content.
  4. Reader features, tests, analytics, weak-topic reporting, and recommendations work as approved.
  5. Students can chat one-to-one with approved mentors and use supported attachments.
  6. Admin can manage content, tests, courses, current affairs, users, and mentor approval.
  7. Razorpay payment, validity-based access, invoices, refunds, coupons, and payment history work according to the approved flow.
  8. AI can use approved indexed content and show available source references/citations.
  9. Security, role restrictions, audit logs, backups, and one-session control are implemented as agreed.
  10. Critical in-scope UAT issues are fixed in one correction cycle.

Not Included

  • Hosting, domain, SSL, storage, model/API, payment gateway, messaging, and other third-party charges
  • Play Store account, publishing, and deployment charges
  • Live classes or live streaming
  • Content creation, proofreading, translation approval, or legal rights clearance
  • Features added after approval without a change request
